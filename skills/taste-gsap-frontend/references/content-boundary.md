# Content provenance

On an existing product, preserve copy, navigation labels, facts, contacts, legal text
and localization unless the task requests changes. Adapt the layout before shortening
text. Animation never justifies inventing claims or changing terminology.

On a fresh website, the commission includes usable copy from the supplied subject,
features and facts. Use the upstream copy references for voice and structure. Do not
turn implementation instructions ("use GSAP", "make it premium") into product claims.
Never invent customers, reviews, certifications, sales figures or guarantees.

Keep a short fact record: supplied, existing, verified source, or missing. Ask only
for facts that block a working result. Mark unavoidable draft facts in the handoff;
do not disguise them as verified. Use real destinations when supplied. A missing
backend is an integration gap, not a successful fake form submission.

Upstream `.example` contacts and assumed hours are draft conventions. They do not
replace authentic supplied details or authorize invented facts for an existing
business. Record an individual checker waiver for an authentic contact if necessary.

Design Read and Motion Map belong in working notes, never visitor-facing text.
Photographs keep licence/source credits. Identify stand-ins in the handoff; never
present them as photographs of this client's actual product, staff or property.
